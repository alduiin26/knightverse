KNIGHTVERSE MANIFESTO

The comprehensive architectural, economic, and technical overview of the next-generation MMORPG ecosystem built on the Binance Smart Chain.

01. Introduction & Vision

KnightVerse is a next-generation, 2D turn-based Play-to-Earn (P2E) RPG built on the Binance Smart Chain (BNB).

Merging a two-decade legacy of MMORPG faction mechanics (Karus vs. Human) with a sustainable Web3 economy, KnightVerse eliminates the fatal flaws of traditional P2E models. By integrating Real Yield mechanics through a B2B E-Pin marketplace and establishing robust deflationary token sinks, KnightVerse delivers a truly scalable, player-centric ecosystem.

02. Core Gameplay & Energy

The ecosystem is designed to respect the player's time while strictly controlling token emission.

  • Daily Stamina: To prevent endless grinding and bot-driven inflation, each NFT character is limited to 3 battles per day.
  • Zero-Penalty Loss: Fostering a healthy growth environment, losing a battle does not destroy items or deduct tokens. The player simply consumes 1 stamina and receives reduced Experience Points (XP).
  • Logarithmic Progression (Token Sink): Characters can reach a maximum of Level 59. Leveling up strictly requires burning $KNIGHT tokens. To counter inflationary rewards at higher levels, the level-up cost scales logarithmically (e.g., Level 1 requires 100 $KNIGHT; Level 40 requires 2,500 $KNIGHT). All costs are fixed in $KNIGHT (No-Oracle Policy).

03. Combat Modes

Solo Arena (1v1): Entry-level PvE combat. Rewards are dynamically generated based on character rarity and win rate.

Tactical Squad (3v3 Arena): Players deploy 3 characters of the exact same rarity. Grants a +30% Synergy Bonus to the baseline reward pool, incentivizing secondary market volume.

World Raids (PvE): Elite characters (Level 25, 40, 55 thresholds) challenge World Bosses in 1v1 combat, offering a 2x reward multiplier and exclusive NFT item drops.

Fair-Play (Anti-P2W): Algorithmic matchmaking pairs players strictly based on NFT rarity and power levels, protecting low-budget players from high-net-worth "whales."

04. NFT Rarity

Characters are categorized into 4 tiers: Common, Rare, Epic, and Legendary. Rarity directly dictates the RNG-based reward pool limits per victory.

Value Retention: When a leveled character is traded on a secondary market (e.g., OpenSea), it retains its exact Level and XP, exponentially increasing floor prices.

Un-socketing Economy: NFT weapons and armors can be detached and sold independently, requiring a $KNIGHT token fee that acts as an additional deflationary sink.

05. Taxation & The Rolling Time-Lock

KnightVerse utilizes a zero-burn, cyclic Treasury system governed by smart contracts:

Withdrawal Tax 5%
DEX Swap Tax 2%

Rolling Time-Lock: Taxes and in-game sinks are deposited into the Protocol Treasury and mathematically locked for 3 months, then strategically deployed for buybacks or subsidies.

06. Real Yield & E-Pins

KnightVerse bridges Web3 liquidity with the massive Web2 traditional gaming market through a proprietary secondary marketplace selling E-Pins.

Stake-to-Discount: Staking $KNIGHT grants access to purchase Real-World E-Pins at a discounted rate (e.g., 5%). Strategic B2B Scaling secures E-Pins at wholesale rates via direct CEO-level partnerships, operating at zero profit margin to pass value directly to stakers.

07. Death Spiral Solution

PvP Wager Rake Mechanism: In competitive PvP, matched players wager equal amounts of $KNIGHT.

The protocol automatically deducts a 10% Rake Fee from the total pool before distributing the rest to the victor, removing millions of tokens from circulation daily.

08 - 10. Security, Access & Minting

Web2 Onboarding

One-Time Account Linking: Players register via Twitter (X) and sign a one-time gas-free signature connecting their Web3 wallet. Play directly from any mobile browser via Twitter login.

Anti-Bot & Whales

Entry requires a Genesis NFT (1-5 USDT), creating an insurmountable cost barrier for bot farms. No Scholarship Program. Permanent LP Lock prior to Mainnet TGE.

Genesis Mint

Genesis NFT purchases feature a 3-day reveal period. Airdrop rewards unlock 40% at TGE, with the remaining 60% distributed linearly over 12 months.
